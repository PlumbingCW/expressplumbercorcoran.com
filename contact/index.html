What to say on the call

Give the address, the problem and anything unsafe. Say if water is running, if sewage is coming up or if there is a gas smell. Mention the water heater brand if you can read the label.

Tell the dispatcher about anything that affects access, such as a gate, a pet or a tenant who is home. That helps the plumber arrive ready.

Have this ready

  • Your street address and nearest cross street.
  • What you see, hear or smell.
  • Whether you shut off water or power.
  • A good phone number to call you back.
