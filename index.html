Emergency plumbing

When a plumbing problem is an emergency

An emergency plumber in Corcoran, CA should answer when water is moving where it should not, when a drain is pushing sewage back into the house or when the water heater has failed and a family has no hot water. These problems damage floors, drywall and belongings every hour they continue.

Our 24/7 emergency line is open every day of the year. Call, describe the problem and we will tell you what to do first, then send a plumber. Many Corcoran homes sit on concrete slabs, so a leak you cannot see can run under the floor for days before a bill or a wet spot gives it away.

Do this first while you wait

  • Shut off the main water valve if water is running or spraying.
  • Stop using sinks, tubs and toilets if a drain is backing up.
  • Turn off the water heater power or gas if the tank is leaking.
  • Keep people and pets away from wet areas near outlets and appliances.
  • Move valuables and rugs away from the water.

Before we arrive

What to do before the plumber arrives

A few quick steps limit damage and help the plumber start fixing the problem the moment the truck pulls up. None of them need tools beyond a flashlight.

Find the main shutoff

It is often at the front of the house near the meter, or on an outside wall. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Stop the drains

If sewage is rising, do not run water, flush or use the dishwasher. More water makes a backup worse.

Photograph the damage

Photos of leaks, stains and the water heater label help the plumber and help with insurance later.

Clear the work area

Move boxes from under sinks and around the water heater so the plumber can reach the problem quickly.

Plumbing in Corcoran, a Kings County city

Corcoran is a Kings County city of about 22,000 people, and Whitley Avenue is its main commercial street. Shops, restaurants and offices along that corridor depend on working restrooms, grease-safe drains and reliable hot water, which is why commercial plumbing sits next to home service on our list. Big local employers such as the J. G. Boswell Company and the two state prisons keep the city busy through every season, and the Amtrak Gold Runner stop connects the town to the rest of the valley. When a pipe fails, that same busy-ness means you want a plumber who is based in town and answers the phone at any hour.

Corcoran's water comes from the city system. The City of Corcoran's 2021 water quality report lists average hardness of 52 ppm, which is soft, so scale is lighter here than in many valley towns. Soft water is kind to heaters and fixtures, though age still wears out valves, seals and tanks.
