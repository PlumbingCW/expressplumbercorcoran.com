Corcoran Express Plumbing dispatches from Whitley Avenue in Corcoran, California. We serve the city first, then Hanford and nearby Kings County communities.

Corcoran, our home base

Corcoran is in Kings County, and our address is 1500 Whitley Ave, ZIP 93212. We serve homes and businesses across the city, including the Whitley Avenue commercial strip. Emergency calls here get the shortest travel time.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

In any emergency in the area, start with the main water valve. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If sewage is rising, stop using sinks, tubs and toilets. If a water heater is leaking, turn off its power or gas and close the cold-water valve.

Then move belongings away from the water, take photos of the damage and keep the phone close so the dispatcher can reach you.

How dispatch works across the area

The dispatcher asks for your address, the problem and any safety concerns. The plumber who is closest and available is sent. For emergencies outside the city, travel time is longer, and the dispatcher tells you honestly what to expect.

We do not promise a fixed arrival time, because roads and call volume change. We do promise to tell you what we know, and to update you if it changes.

Corcoran, Hanford and the roads between them

Corcoran and Hanford are linked by State Route 43, which runs north through farm country to Hanford, and the drive is about 17 miles. Most calls in the area come from homes and shops in those two cities, so the dispatcher plans around them first.

Places west of Corcoran, such as Kettleman City, sit farther from our base. The dispatcher will tell you honestly how long a visit is likely to take, and whether it makes sense to schedule instead of waiting for an emergency slot.

If you manage several properties across the area, such as rentals or small shops, call once and describe them. A single point of contact makes follow-up simpler.

Services across the area

The same services are available across the area, from drain cleaning and water heater replacement to leak detection and commercial plumbing. The full list is on the services index.

Planned work, such as repiping or installation, is scheduled for a time that suits you. Emergencies are handled right away.

Water systems differ by town

The City of Corcoran publishes a yearly water quality report, and the City of Hanford publishes its own. Both are good places to check the source and the tested results for your address. Properties outside city limits may use a different provider or a private well.

If you are not sure who supplies your water, ask the provider listed on your bill.
