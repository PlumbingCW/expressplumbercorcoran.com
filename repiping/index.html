Repiping in Corcoran, CA

When pipes keep leaking or water comes out brown and weak, patching stops making sense. We plan repiping around your home and your schedule.

☎ Call (661) 338-1180

Repiping in Corcoran, CA means replacing the water supply lines inside your home, from the main shutoff to each fixture. It is planned work, and the goal is to stop repeated leaks, restore pressure and remove old pipe that is wearing out from the inside.

A full repipe replaces nearly every supply line. A partial repipe replaces one section, such as a bathroom group, a kitchen run or the line to a single wing of the house. The plumber should show you why a full or partial plan fits your pipe, not just recommend the larger job.

How to tell the pipes are near the end

Frequent pinhole leaks, rust-colored water at the tap, low pressure that has slowly worsened and visible green corrosion at copper joints are the usual signs. In older homes with galvanized steel, the inside of the pipe narrows with rust until flow drops.

One leak does not mean the whole house needs new pipe. Three leaks in a year in different places is a different story. The plumber looks at the pipe material, the age of the home and the repair history before recommending a plan.

Pipe materials: copper, PEX and what is already in your walls

Homes built in different decades used different materials. Galvanized steel was common in older houses. Copper became standard later. PEX is a flexible plastic pipe now widely used for supply lines. Each has strengths and limits.

Copper lasts a long time but can pinhole in aggressive water. PEX is quick to install and resists freezing damage, though it must be protected from sunlight. The plumber will recommend a material based on code, cost and your home.

  • The year the house was built and any past pipe repairs.
  • Photos of the pipe where it is visible near the heater and main.
  • Notes on rusty water, low pressure or leak locations.
  • Plans for remodeling that might share the same wall openings.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Close the stop valve under the fixture or behind the toilet.
  • Turn off power to a garbage disposal before any work.
  • Never put a hand into a disposal, even when it is off.
  • Clear the area under the sink so the plumber can reach the pipes.
  • Note when the problem started and what changed.

Water quality in Corcoran and your pipes

The City of Corcoran's 2021 water quality report shows soft groundwater, with average hardness of 52 ppm and total dissolved solids averaging 240 ppm. Soft water can be gentler on scale but may be more corrosive to some metals than hard water.

If your home has older copper or galvanized pipe, a plumber can look at pipe condition rather than guessing about the cause. The city's annual report and your own tap test are the places to check water quality.

Cast iron drains and what repiping does not cover

Repiping covers pressurized supply lines. Waste and drain lines are a different system. Older homes may have cast iron drain pipes that corrode and flake, and those are repaired or replaced separately.

If your house has both supply and drain problems, the plumber can plan them in an order that reduces wall openings. For waste lines see sewer line repair, and for hidden supply leaks start with leak detection.

Code and licensing for fixture and piping work

Fixture and piping work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). It sets rules for traps, vents, fixture clearances and approved materials, and it is the standard a plumbing inspector checks against.

Minor repairs often need no permit, while new fixtures, relocated drains and whole-house piping usually do. Ask the City of Corcoran building department about your project.
