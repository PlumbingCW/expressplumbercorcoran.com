Grease traps and kitchen drains

A grease trap catches fats and oils before they enter the sewer. When a trap fills, the kitchen drain slows and backs up. Traps need regular pumping and cleaning, and the schedule depends on the volume of cooking.

We can clear the drain lines and use hydro jetting on grease-coated pipe. Large trap pump-outs may require a licensed hauler, and we will say so up front if your trap needs one.

Kitchen plumbing checklist for owners

  • Know where the grease trap or interceptor is and how often it is serviced.
  • Keep a log of pump-out dates.
  • Use drain screens at every sink and floor drain.
  • Never pour fryer oil down any drain.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Close the nearest shutoff and tell staff to avoid the area.

  • Photograph the damage for the owner or insurer.
  • Have the landlord or manager contact ready.

Backflow devices and water quality

Backflow preventers protect the public water supply from contamination when pressure drops. Some businesses are required to test them on a schedule, and the testing must be done by a certified tester.

If your business has a backflow device, ask the City of Corcoran water department what testing schedule applies. We can inspect the plumbing around the device and help you understand what is needed.

Landlords, tenants and property managers

For rentals, tell us who approves the work. We prefer a clear authorization from the owner or manager so the repair can start without delay.

Keep a written record of the date, the problem, the work done and the photos. Those notes help with insurance, tenant disputes and future maintenance. If you manage several units, ask about a single point of contact for repeat issues.

Code and licensing for commercial plumbing

Commercial plumbing follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), plus local requirements for backflow protection and grease interceptors where they apply. Tenant improvements and new fixtures generally need permits.

Confirm what applies to your building with the City of Corcoran building department, and ask for a plumber's license number before work starts so you can verify it.
