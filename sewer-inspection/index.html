Sewer Camera Inspection in Corcoran, CA

A camera shows what is wrong inside the pipe so you decide with facts. Book a sewer camera inspection for your Corcoran property.

☎ Call (661) 338-1180

A sewer camera inspection in Corcoran, CA sends a small waterproof camera through your sewer line and records what it sees. You get a real look at roots, cracks, bellies, offset joints and buildup instead of a plumber's guess.

Inspections are used before buying a home, after a repeat clog, before a repair and as a regular check on an older house. They are planned work, so you can schedule a visit that suits your week.

When a camera inspection earns its cost

Buyers use inspections to check the sewer lateral before closing. A home inspector looks at the house, but rarely at the buried pipe between the house and the street. A camera shows the condition of that pipe.

Owners with repeat clogs use inspections to find the cause. If a line backs up twice in a year, the camera answers the question that another snaking cannot: is the pipe damaged or just dirty?

How an inspection runs

01Access

The camera is fed through the outside cleanout or a pulled toilet.

02Record

The plumber records the full run from house to main.

03Locate

Problem points are located and marked on the ground.

04Review

You watch the key clips and get a plain summary and options.

What the camera can and cannot see

The camera shows the inside surface of the pipe, roots at joints, cracks, scale, offsets and low spots holding water. With a locator, the plumber can mark the depth and position on the ground so a repair can be planned.

A camera cannot see through a total blockage, and it does not test the strength of the pipe. If water or debris blocks the view, the line may need cleaning first with drain cleaning or sewer line cleaning.

What you receive after the visit

  • The video of the line and a plain-language summary.
  • Marks or notes for the depth and location of problems.
  • A recommendation: monitor, clean, line or repair.
  • Photos or clips you can share with a seller, landlord or insurer.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets and the dishwasher.
  • Do not pour chemical drain openers into a backed-up line.
  • Keep children and pets away from standing sewage.
  • Wash hands after any contact with wastewater.
  • Move rugs and stored items away from floor drains.

What the camera may show and what it means

Findings guide the next step. They are not a diagnosis of the entire system.

Corcoran homes that benefit most

Homes on older lots with mature trees, properties with a history of backups and houses where the original sewer pipe is still in service benefit most. Newer homes with plastic pipe still gain from a baseline picture.

Corcoran is on the former Tulare Lake bed, and the area is known for slow ground movement. A camera shows slope problems that lead to standing water in the line and repeated clogs.

Planning a repair from the video

If the camera shows damage, the next step is a repair plan. The plumber marks the problem on the ground and compares spot repair, lining and replacement, which is explained on the sewer line repair page.

If the video shows a healthy pipe, you have a record that supports a sale or insurance conversation. Either way, you know what is under the yard.

Code and licensing for drain and sewer work

Drain and sewer work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). The code covers cleanouts, pipe slope, trap and vent rules and approved materials, which is why a repair that replaces pipe may need a permit and an inspection.

The line in the street belongs to the city, and the lateral on your property usually belongs to you, though boundaries vary. Ask Corcoran Public Works through the City of Corcoran website where the city's part begins for your address.

Source type: state licensing board (Contractors State License Board license check), California Plumbing Code (UPC-based), city government website (City of Corcoran). Rules can change, so confirm current requirements.
